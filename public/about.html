How CARELINK works

Everything a judge, hospital or patient might ask, in one page.

The emergency in seven steps

Identify→Assess→Match→Route→Alert→Prepare→Arrive

The bystander needs no account. Patients, guardians and hospital staff each have a login and a dashboard; their actions feed back into the bystander's live screen.

Triage (rule engine)

Critical if the patient is not conscious or not breathing, or any of: unconscious, unresponsive, no pulse, cardiac arrest, severe bleeding, chest pain, collapsed, seizure, stroke, choking, overdose, head injury.

Urgent for: fracture, burn, high fever, confusion, difficulty breathing, deep cut, severe pain, allergic reaction and similar.

Stable otherwise. An optional AI model may refine the result, but it can never lower a Critical rating, has a 5 s timeout, and any failure falls back to the rules. It is an advisory estimate, not a diagnosis.

Privacy and safety

  • Medical data only with the patient's consent; they can withdraw guardian alerts any time.
  • A QR code holds a random token, never medical data. Responders see only an emergency summary.
  • Every lookup is logged and visible to the patient in their Access log.
  • False alarms: a 5 second cancel window, a "False alarm" button that notifies everyone, and rate limits on lookups.
  • Passwords are hashed (scrypt); sessions use HttpOnly cookies; each API checks the caller's role.
